# The factory collection {#the-factory-collection}

The 27 factory blocks share a consistent grid and material palette. They are made at the [Foundry Workbench](workbench.md).

- [Floors and walkways](architecture.md): plates, concrete, drainage, stairs, catwalks and structural details.
- [Doors, shutters and lighting](mechanisms.md): owned mechanical controls and saved open/closed or light states.

See [recipes](../recipes.md) for quantities and [configuration and access](../configuration.md) for ownership rules. Crafting, building, controls, normal save/reload, sounds and multiplayer were included in player testing. Keep backups while using the alpha.
