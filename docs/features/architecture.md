# Factory architecture

## Connected floors

**Riveted floor plate**, **Safety edge floor**, **Poured factory concrete** and **Recessed drain grate** are full-height floor blocks. Safety floor automatically removes shared border stripes and keeps an exposed outline, including L-shaped corners. Grated catwalk variants and drain grates remove shared edge frames beside compatible grates. Removing a neighbour restores the exposed edge.

## Catwalks and stairs

Choose open, left-rail, right-rail, both-rail or outer-corner catwalks. Adjacent walkways remove their shared edge frames and internal side rails. A stair flight rises one block over a one-block run. Place flights side by side, facing the same way, to create wider stairs with the internal handrails removed. Place the top landing one block higher in the next cell beyond the highest tread. A catwalk or a full block can fill this landing.

Grated decks use continuous walking collision so feet cannot fall into the small visual gaps. Independent **Safety railing** has a visible placement preview. Use **R** to check its direction before placing.

## Walls, windows and roof

**Steel factory wall** is a full block with the same sheet-metal cladding and seam texture on all six faces. Leave a one-block-wide, two-block-high opening for a **Factory personnel door**. The door has matching cladding and a glazed opening on both sides. **Corrugated factory roof** is a full block; rotate its ribs to match the rest of the roof.

**Steel-framed factory window** has a full-depth frame. **Industrial window pane** is a thin alternative for internal partitions. Both have clear glazing, wire reinforcement and a steel surround. Rotate before placing.

Mount a **High-bay industrial pendant** beneath the roof for large warehouse lighting. Use **F** to switch it. The guarded wall lamp remains available for corridors and work areas.

## Ducts, vents and pallets

Utility ducts use the native pipe rotation controls for vertical and horizontal runs. Use **R** to select the axis before placing. Straight sections and elbows have matching ports centred on block faces. They are architectural fittings and do not transport fluids, items or energy.

Place an elbow against the straight duct's end face, then use **R** to turn its other port toward the next section. Elbows support all twelve perpendicular face pairs, including turns in a horizontal run. This placement change is undergoing player verification in the repair preview.

**Louvred wall vents** join horizontally and vertically when facing the same direction. Use them to make larger rectangular vents. **Steel cargo pallets** join along shared edges, including a two-by-two square; forklift pockets remain beneath the deck.

Columns and crossbeams form matching steel frames. Keep moving mechanisms clear. See [recipes](../recipes.md) and [mechanisms](mechanisms.md).
