# Floors and walkways

## Main floor

**Riveted floor plate**, **Safety edge floor**, **Poured factory concrete** and **Recessed drain grate** form full-height floor blocks. Align the safety strip with a loading-bay edge or change in height. The grate is decorative drainage; it does not collect or move fluid.

## Catwalks

The open catwalk is a thin grated deck. Choose left rail, right rail or both rails for straight runs. The outer-corner piece protects a right-angle landing. A stair flight rises one block over a one-block run; repeat it with landings for taller floors.

The fine visual gaps in grated decks use a continuous walking surface in the collision definitions. Stair rails use conservative collision envelopes. Native walking and rotation checks are still pending for this preview.

## Structure and fittings

Stack bolted I-section columns and join them with one-block crossbeams. Independent safety rails and guide extensions provide matching details. Straight utility ducts and elbows have matching ports, but **do not transport fluids, items or energy**. Wall vents and cargo pallets are decorative blocks.

Rotate directional pieces before placing. Large items require their complete occupied cells to be empty and loaded. Keep the movement space around nearby mechanisms clear.
