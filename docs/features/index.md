# The factory collection

The 25 factory blocks share a consistent grid and material palette. They are made at the [Foundry Workbench](workbench.md).

- [Floors and walkways](architecture.md): plates, concrete, drainage, stairs, catwalks and structural details.
- [Doors, shutters and lighting](mechanisms.md): owned mechanical controls and saved open/closed or light states.
- [Freight lift and call panels](lift.md): experimental three-block passenger lift with a separate controller.

See [recipes](../recipes.md) for quantities and [configuration and access](../configuration.md) for ownership rules. Asset loading is verified; native placement, operation and multiplayer acceptance are ongoing.
