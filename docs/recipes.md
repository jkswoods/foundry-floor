# Recipes

These are the recipes for 0.1.0. Native server transactions have verified the bench and all 25 factory input/output quantities, including conserved remainders and wrong-bench refusal. Complete player UI and crafting-time verification remain in progress.

## Foundry Workbench

At a standard **Workbench**, combine **8 Iron Ingots + 4 Copper Ingots** to make **1 Foundry Workbench**.

## Factory blocks

Every recipe below uses **Iron Ingots** at the Foundry Workbench and takes **2 seconds**. The Makes column is the output per craft. There is no fuel or power cost.
